# Emberleaf Isle

An island of tree-top mages, meadow archers, cliff warriors and harbor rogues, split by the Stonebrow cliffs and joined by the Glimmerrun river.

- Long ago the five founders sealed the Ashen Sorcerer beneath Emberleaf; the seal holds while the towns keep their oaths.
- Every ship that reaches Emberleaf docks at Saltwake Harbor; there is no other safe landing.
- The Glimmerrun rises at Sylvarch's spring and runs past Ambermeadow to the sea at Saltwake; barges carry goods and gossip down it in a day.
- Stonebrow folk trust their own and half believe any news from below the cliffs.
- Saltmarch lives on trade; what the harbor does not tax, the Lantern Guild moves through Gutterlight's canals.
- Archmage Elowen keeps the founders' sealing wards in Sylvarch's library; only apprentices who pass the Trial may read them.
- The spring under the great tree has glowed faintly every night this month, and the mages will not say why.
- Ambermeadow's mushroom fields feed half the island; the harvest comes in before the first frost.
- Tobbin found grey spots on the caps in his east field and burned a whole row.
- Captain Brisa's archers still watch the old cliff road, closed since the rockslide.
- Ironcrag's warriors swear an oath at the Great Forge before they may carry a blade outside the town.
- The cliff road down to Ambermeadow fell three winters ago; Ironcrag has only the long Dustroad to the lowlands now.
- The diggers have reached walls older than the founders, carved with rings like those on the sealing wards.
- Foreman Rusk stopped the night shift after two diggers heard knocking from inside the rock.
- Newcomers step off the ferry at Saltwake and give their names to Harbormaster Olin.
- Pell's ferry crosses to the mainland every third day, weather allowing.
- The harbor raised the salt tax this season, and the fishwives say it will rise again.
- The Lantern Guild runs Gutterlight's night markets and decides which lanterns stay lit.
- Vex's lieutenants quarrel over the canal tolls; two lantern districts went dark this week.
- Warden Ysolde guards the gate to the caverns below Hollowdeep; no one goes down without her leave.
- Old Fenn says the caverns breathe when the seal weakens, and that they breathed last week.
- Sunstrand's tide pools are full of glowing shells, sold as charms in Saltwake.
- Coral guides visitors along the beach and knows every wreck on the shore.

- Archmage Elowen, mage elder of Sylvarch: Keeper of the founders' wards; speaks slowly and answers questions with questions.
- Pip, apprentice mage of Sylvarch: An apprentice who has not passed the Trial and talks too much about the library.
- Captain Brisa, archer captain of Ambermeadow: Leads the archers who watch the fallen cliff road.
- Tobbin, mushroom farmer of Ambermeadow: Farms the east fields and worries about the harvest.
- Chief Durgan, warrior chief of Ironcrag: Ironcrag's chief, who swore the forge oath forty winters ago.
- Ketta, blacksmith of Ironcrag: Tends the Great Forge and hears every rumor that comes up the Dustroad.
- Foreman Rusk, dig foreman of Ashen Dig: Runs the dig and keeps the diggers' fears out of his reports.
- Harbormaster Olin, harbormaster of Saltwake Harbor: Writes down every newcomer's name and every ship's cargo.
- Pell, ferry captain of Saltwake Harbor: Runs the ferry to the mainland and carries letters for a coin.
- Nessa, innkeeper of Saltwake Harbor: Keeps the Tide Lantern inn, where travellers trade news.
- Vex, guild master of Gutterlight: Master of the Lantern Guild, polite and never fully honest.
- Marlo, fence of Gutterlight: Buys and sells whatever the canals bring, no questions asked.
- Warden Ysolde, gate warden of Hollowdeep: Guards the deep gate and trusts no one who wants to go down.
- Old Fenn, hermit of Hollowdeep: A hermit who listens to the caverns and is rarely believed.
- Coral, beach guide of Sunstrand: Guides visitors along the shore and collects glowing shells.
